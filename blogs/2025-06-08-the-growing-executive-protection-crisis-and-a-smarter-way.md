Every time a high-profile leader steps outside the office, the risk landscape shifts. Security teams have long relied on proven safeguards—badge readers, lobby controls, perimeter fencing, security detail—but those measures only cover part of the picture. Today’s threats don’t wait at the gate—they travel with your people.
